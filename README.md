# My Favorite Movie: Avengers: Endgame

## Overview
Avengers: Endgame is a superhero film produced by Marvel Studios.  
It is the final part of the Infinity Saga and shows how the Avengers come together to defeat Thanos.

---

## Main Characters
1. Iron Man (Tony Stark)  
2. Captain America (Steve Rogers)  
3. Thor  
4. Black Widow  
5. Hulk  
6. Hawkeye

---

## Why I Like This Movie
- The story is exciting and emotional.  
- It has amazing action scenes.  
- It shows teamwork and sacrifice.  

> "Whatever it takes." - Captain America
